## [0.1.0] - unreleased

### Added

- **widgets:** on this day (selected anniversaries, events, births, deaths or holidays), picture of the day of Wikimedia Commons with its author and licence, article of the day (else the most read one of yesterday), most read articles, each in the Wikipedia of the language of the dashboard (English, French, German, Spanish)
- **device:** today's anniversary, article and picture as texts for dashboards and scenes
- **scenes:** actions "on this day", "picture of the day" and "article of the day", with texts ready to send or to speak
- **configuration:** language of the device and the scene actions, "Read Wikipedia now" button; no account and no key
- **i18n:** English, French, German and Spanish for the manifest, the runtime texts, the documentation and the README
